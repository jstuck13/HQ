Restore

putting a page back the way it was

From a backup

Every document is copied nightly and thirty days are kept. Pick the day you want back, then pick only the pages that went wrong — the rest of HQ carries on untouched. Whatever is replaced is itself copied first, so this is as undoable as the thing it undoes.

    Backups are made by the nightly sync; you can run one at any time from the bell.
