What fills these pages

Nothing here is typed twice. Each of these runs on its own and writes into one document; what each last managed is below, read from the same ledger the bell reads.

    This copy

      Where everything is keptone document each

      Every page keeps its state as one JSON document in a Postgres table, with a copy in this browser so the pages open with no signal. These are the keys.

        What HQ does not do

        • It does not talk to a bank or a broker. Holdings and transactions are yours to enter; HQ keeps the arithmetic.
        • It does not decide anything for you. Nothing here is advice, and a pattern it finds is two things moving together, never a cause.
        • It does not reach a service you have not given it. Canvas, Google and Garmin are reached with your own credentials, kept as documents beginning _, which the API will never serve back.
        • It does not keep your data anywhere but your own database and your own browser. There is no third party.
        • It is built for one person. There are no accounts, no sharing, and one passcode.
